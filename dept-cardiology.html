Our Services

Cardiology Services

Comprehensive cardiac care using state-of-the-art technology and evidence-based treatment protocols.

📊

ECG & Echocardiography

Advanced electrocardiogram and echo imaging to assess heart function, structure, and blood flow.

🩺

Stress Testing

Treadmill and pharmacological stress tests to evaluate heart performance under controlled conditions.

🔬

Minimally invasive procedure to diagnose and treat coronary artery disease and blockages.

💊

⚡

Pacemaker Implantation

Implantation and management of pacemakers and defibrillators for rhythm disorders.

🏥

Supervised recovery programs post-surgery or heart attack to restore strength and prevent recurrence.

Conditions We Treat

Common Heart Conditions

Our team is experienced in diagnosing and managing a full spectrum of cardiovascular conditions.

Coronary Artery Disease

Narrowing of coronary arteries reducing blood supply to the heart muscle.

Heart Failure

Condition where the heart doesn't pump blood efficiently enough to meet body's needs.

Arrhythmia

Irregular heartbeat patterns including atrial fibrillation and ventricular tachycardia.

Hypertension

High blood pressure management to reduce risk of stroke and heart attack.

Valvular Heart Disease

Diseases affecting the heart valves including stenosis and regurgitation.

Emergency Heart Care

Our cardiac emergency unit is operational 24/7 with rapid response teams for heart attacks and acute cardiac events.

  • 24/7 Cardiac ICU
  • Rapid Angioplasty (Door-to-Balloon <90 min)
  • Dedicated Chest Pain Unit
  • Advanced Life Support Teams
  • Helicopter Emergency Services
