Conditions We Treat

Neurological Conditions

Our specialists manage a full range of brain and nervous system disorders.

Stroke & TIA

Emergency and preventive care for brain attacks and transient ischemic attacks.

Epilepsy & Seizures

Comprehensive epilepsy management with medication optimization and surgery when needed.

Parkinson's Disease

Movement disorder management including medication, DBS, and physiotherapy programs.

Multiple Sclerosis

Disease-modifying therapies and symptom management for MS patients.

Migraine & Headache

Chronic headache evaluation and preventive treatment protocols.

Stroke Emergency Unit

Our dedicated stroke unit provides 24/7 rapid response. Golden hour treatment dramatically improves outcomes.

  • 24/7 Stroke Response Team
  • IV Thrombolysis Available
  • Mechanical Thrombectomy
  • Dedicated Neuro ICU
  • Post-Stroke Rehabilitation
